Liquor Ball Sandwiches

A quick and easy breakfast with only a couple of ingredients and a reasonable amount of labour.

Ingredients

  • 1 bottle of your favourite liquor — preferably vodka
  • 2 slices of bread, white

Steps

  1. Remove the 4 crusts from your slice of bread with a knife. If you're not scared keep them on.
  2. Generously douse the uncrusted bread with your preferred alcoholic liquid.
  3. By hand, scrunch and roll the soggy bread into small balls.
  4. Consume and enjoy.
