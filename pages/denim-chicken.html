Denim Chicken

A cooked chicken with denim garnish.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon onion powder, or to taste
  • half a cup butter
  • 1 pair of made in USA blue Levi's 501 Shrink-to-Fit jeans

Steps

  1. Gather all the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 175°C or 350°F.
  2. Place chicken in a roasting pan, season the chicken with onion powder, salt and pepper. Insert 3 tablespoons of butter inside of the chicken. Place the rest of the butter on top of the chicken.
  3. Bake the chicken in the preheated oven until good and the juices run clear, roughly 1 hour and 15 minutes.
  4. Cover with aluminum foil and rest for 30 minutes.
  5. Once rested, place the chicken inside of the pair of made in USA blue Levi's 501 Shrink-to-Fit jeans and sit upright.
